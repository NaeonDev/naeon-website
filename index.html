Everything your operation runs on

Switch on the modules you need and leave the rest off. Every company builds the NAEON that fits the way it works.

Sales

Agents, customers, products, quote requests and orders. Requests go to the sales manager and management for sign-off.

Production

Plan and track production runs from start to finish, including quality checks and what's in progress right now.

Stock

Raw materials and finished goods, with low-stock warnings before they turn into a problem.

Delivery & fleet

Deliveries with proof of delivery, kilometres travelled by reps while clocked in, and fuel slips captured by photo.

Finance tracking

Approved quote figures, purchase order approvals, payments and invoice status, alongside the systems you already use.

People & security

Employee onboarding with personal sign-in codes, HR and policy files, and gate visitor logs that notify reception.
