5. Third-party error monitoring

The app uses Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) for technical error reports, not advertising, analytics, session recording or performance tracking. NAEON’s configured reporting removes names, email addresses, telephone numbers, login IDs, IP addresses, cookies and request headers before transmission. Technical error information is sent to Sentry when an error occurs. The app serves its fonts from NAEON’s own server and loads no other third-party scripts.

Maps in the app load map data and map fonts from NAEON’s own storage (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors); no third-party map service is contacted. When a driver presses Navigate, the stop is opened in the driver’s own maps app (for example Google Maps or Waze) under that provider’s terms. Where a user switches on push notifications, the browser registers with its own push service (for example Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft) to receive them; the content is encrypted. The customer track-and-trace page sets no cookies.

6. Choices and consent

The app’s listed technologies are necessary for core functions or support user-requested preferences and notifications. No non-essential advertising or analytics cookies are deployed, so a separate tracking-consent banner is not used for this configuration. Users may block or clear cookies and storage in browser settings, but doing so may sign them out, remove settings, prevent features from working or delete unsent offline work. Push notifications can be switched off in app, browser or device settings.
