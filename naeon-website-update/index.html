The platform

One app for every department

Most companies run on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and paper slips. NAEON replaces the chasing with one place where work is captured, approved and followed through.

Sales & CRM

Know every customer, every quote, every promise

Leads, customers, quote requests and orders in one place, with the full story of each customer on a single timeline.

  • Quote requests signed off by the sales manager or management
  • Price agreements, complaints and a contact log per customer
  • RFQs sent to finance land with the right rep automatically
  • Monthly targets, visit goals and follow-up reminders
Approvals

Approvals that don't wait for someone to be at their desk

Leave, expenses, quotes, purchases and visitors all arrive in one inbox, on any device. Approve in a tap, or several at once.

  • Nobody can approve their own request
  • Bulk approve routine items, with flagged ones held back
  • Withdraw, fix and resubmit, showing exactly what changed
Stock, dispatch & delivery

From the warehouse shelf to the customer's signature

Raw materials and finished goods, purchase orders, picking, loading and delivery, with low-stock warnings before they cost you a sale.

  • Purchase requests, supplier quotes and goods received
  • Dispatch board, picking lists and truck load checks
  • Proof of delivery captured on the driver's phone
Field team & the gate

Reps on the road, visitors at the gate, all accounted for

Kilometres travelled while clocked in, fuel slips photographed on the spot, and a gate that knows who's on site and whether they're allowed in.

  • Rep tracking only while clocked in, with visit plans
  • Fuel slips and mileage captured per vehicle
  • Visitors, contractors, trucks and collections at the gate
  • Permits to work, safety registers and roll call in one tap
Production & quality

The factory floor, on a shared tablet

Operators tap in with their employee number and PIN. Each job shows its steps, work instructions and current drawing, and output, scrap and downtime go in as they happen. Output and scrap are kept even when the Wi-Fi drops.

  • Production jobs, routings and capacity per line and shift
  • QC inspections, quality holds and corrective actions
  • Breakdowns, work orders and preventive maintenance
  • Batch and serial traceability from receiving to dispatch

Questions

Things people usually ask

Does NAEON replace our accounting system?

No. NAEON runs alongside it. Your team captures and approves work in NAEON, and finance uses the approved figures in your own accounting system. Tax invoices, credit notes and statements stay there.

Can we start with only a few modules?

Yes. Every feature beyond the core is an optional add-on. Start small, and switch more on when you're ready. You only pay for what you use.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. NAEON was designed for phones first, for reps, drivers, security and factory staff, and it works just as well on tablets and computers. Factory floors can share a tablet, with each person tapping in with a PIN.

How do our staff get access?

You send us your staff list on our template and we create the accounts. Office staff receive an email invitation; factory and warehouse staff get a personal activation code. Management sign in with two-factor authentication.

Is our data safe?

Every company's data is kept strictly separate, and each person only sees what their role allows. You choose how long records are kept, sensitive views are recorded, and every change is in the trail.

Are you tracking our reps and drivers all the time?

No. Reps are tracked only while they're clocked in, and drivers only while a trip is running, and only when your company switches the add-on on. Before anyone is tracked, they read and acknowledge a tracking notice in the app.

What if there's no signal?

The screens used where the signal drops keep working: the gate, clocking in and out, production output, receiving, and drivers' load updates and proof of delivery. Entries are saved on the device and sent the moment there's signal.

Where is our data kept?

In London, United Kingdom, with every company's data kept apart in the database. Our Privacy Policy lists every service provider we use.

Do you use AI on our data?

Only if you switch it on. AI fuel-slip reading, Ask NAEON and the weekly summary are optional add-ons that your administrator accepts first, and a person always checks what AI suggests. AI is never on during a trial.

Can we try it ourselves?

Yes. After a demo we can give your team a 7-day trial: your own copy of a demo company with sample data and every role, at app.naeon.co.za/try.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your team size and the modules you choose. Get in touch and we'll put together a quote for your company.
